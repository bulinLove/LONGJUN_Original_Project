What to review before ordering

Evaluate the material using the proposed batch’s documents and sample. General product descriptions do not replace batch-specific analysis.

Appearance & aroma

The supplied technical record describes a pale-yellow liquid with a characteristic rose aroma. Confirm the proposed batch through sample evaluation.

Physical-property review

Review relative density, refractive index and optical rotation with the test method and temperature stated in the agreed specification.

Composition analysis / GC–MS

For formulation review, request chromatographic information covering citronellol, geraniol, linalool and methyl eugenol where available. Confirm the method, units and batch link before using numerical results.

Pesticide screening

Available screening report; scope, detection limits and sample identity are checked for the intended evaluation.
