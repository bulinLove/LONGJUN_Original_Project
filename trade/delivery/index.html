Trade & Delivery

Packaging & delivery

Align the material, packaging and destination before shipment. Record the agreed responsibilities in the order confirmation.

01

Packaging & identification

Confirm container type, pack size, seals and label requirements. Keep the material and batch references consistent with the documents.

02

Logistics & insurance

Agree the delivery terms, destination, transport requirements, lead time and insurance responsibilities in writing. Confirm the current SDS for transport review.

03

Shipping documents & receipt

Confirm the invoice, packing list and any agreed origin documents. On receipt, compare the shipment with the order and record any issue promptly.
