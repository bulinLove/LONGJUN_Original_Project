Trade with clarity.At every step.

A considered ingredient purchase starts with a shared understanding. Our process connects your brief, the material, its documents and the agreed delivery, so each decision has a clear basis.

What makes the process clear

Material-specific evidence

From first enquiry to continued supply.

  1. 01

    Share your brief

    Tell us the exact material, intended application, destination and expected quantity. Add your target specification, packaging preferences and required timing so the discussion begins with the right scope.

    Record to agree

    A written brief and the questions still to resolve.

  2. 02

    Review the material and documents

    Align the material identity, form and target specification. Review the relevant product and batch documents that are available, and identify any additional information needed for your application and destination.

    Record to agree

    The proposed specification and a list of available or pending documents.

  3. 03

    Evaluate a representative sample

    Agree sample availability, quantity and delivery arrangements. Evaluate the sample in your own application and discuss its relationship to the proposed commercial batch, including any differences that need review.

    Record to agree

    Sample identification, evaluation feedback and the basis for batch acceptance.

  4. 04

    Confirm the order in writing

    Review the quotation and confirm product specification, quantity, packaging, price, payment schedule, delivery responsibilities and expected timing. Record changes before the order proceeds.

    Record to agree

    An agreed quotation or order confirmation with the applicable terms.

  5. 05

    Prepare and dispatch

    Before shipment, review the agreed batch identification, labels, packaging and required shipping documents. Confirm the dispatch arrangements and share shipment details as they become available.

    Record to agree

    The agreed dispatch details and shipment document set.

  6. 06

    Review receipt and plan the next order

    Check packaging, quantity and batch identification on arrival. Share evaluation results or any discrepancy with the relevant batch and shipment details; use the findings to refine the next order.

    Record to agree

    Receipt feedback, any agreed follow-up and updated repeat-order requirements.

Documents, connected to the material.

A specification or TDS describes the proposed material; a COA relates to a particular batch; an SDS supports safety review. The relevant document set, its availability and any further testing needs are confirmed for each enquiry. Review the actual documents before making a purchase decision.
